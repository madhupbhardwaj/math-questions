Purpose

Most problem banks optimise for volume — thousands of questions, endless drilling, a race to the next one. This isn't that. problemset is small on purpose.

Every question here earned its place because I found it genuinely interesting to think about, not just to solve. The goal is focused practice, not endless scrolling.

The problems worth keeping are the ones that ask you to think outside the general criteria of problem solving.
Philosophy

A good problem doesn't just test whether you know a formula — it asks you to see something. To notice a structure, reframe a question, or find the one observation that makes everything else fall into place.

Those are the problems collected here: ones that reward a shift in perspective rather than a memorised procedure.

How it works

Questions are organised by topic — number theory, trigonometry, calculus, combinatorics, and algebra — each with its own colour. Click any question to reveal its answer; where a worked video solution exists, you'll find a link to it.

You can filter by difficulty, search within a topic, share a direct link to any single problem, or copy a question as a clean image to send to a friend.

Scope

The set is currently pitched at the undergraduate level, covering five topics. It grows as I do — I add problems as my own mathematics deepens.
